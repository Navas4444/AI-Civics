AI Technology Behind the System

At the core of the Civic Platform lies a state-of-the-art vision and text architecture. When you upload a picture, our visual CNN (Convolutional Neural Network) segments the scene to find targeted hazards (e.g. broken asphalt vs simple debris) and correlates that image with historical database items.

In parallel, natural language models process voice-to-text transcripts, mining context indicators to generate urgency priority levels automatically. A custom municipal dashboard aggregates these inputs to show thermal heatmaps of systemic urban decay, allowing municipal crews to target prevention programs preemptively.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how our AI systems support smart city maintenance.

How does the AI determine report priority?

Our custom AI vision model parses uploaded images to detect specific parameters, like pothole size or water accumulation depth. Based on hazard classes, it calculates priority indices (Low, Medium, High) to route life-threatening issues first.

What if the AI makes a categorization mistake?

Every report has a built-in human verification step. Municipal queue managers can instantly re-categorize or adjust priority tags with a single click, which in turn helps retrain the AI models for future accuracy.

Can I file reports without sharing my exact GPS coords?

Yes, you can manually type a nearby landmark, street name, or intersection instead of utilizing automated GPS lock. However, GPS coordinates speed up crew routing accuracy.
